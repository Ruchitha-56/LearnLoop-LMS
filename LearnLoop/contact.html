Frequently asked questions

Do I need an account?

You can browse courses without one. To enroll, learn and take quizzes you need a free account, which is created and stored in your own browser.

Where is my progress saved?

Accounts and progress are saved in this browser only (LocalStorage). If you clear your browser data or switch device, they will not follow you.

Is my account password safe?

Passwords are scrambled before saving, but accounts live only in your browser, so this is not a secure service. Do not reuse an important password.

Are the courses free?

Yes. All sample courses and quizzes are free to use.

How do I reset my progress?

Open My Learning and use the “Reset my progress” button at the bottom of the page.

Can I retake a quiz?

Yes, as many times as you like. We keep your best score.
