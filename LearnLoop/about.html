Learning works best as a loop

LearnLoop is a small, friendly place to learn new skills at your own pace. No sign-up, no pressure, just steady progress.

Who we are

LearnLoop began with a simple idea: people finish what they can see themselves making progress on. So every course is split into short lessons, every lesson can be ticked off, and every course ends with a quiz that tells you what stuck.

The site is built for beginners, curious people and anyone returning to learning after a break.

Our mission

To make learning feel doable: clear lessons, honest feedback and visible progress for everyone.

Our vision

A world where anyone can pick up a new skill in their spare minutes and see it grow into something they are proud of.

Our learning philosophy

Small pieces beat big leaps

Ten-minute lessons are easy to start and easy to repeat. Consistency matters more than long sessions.

Check, then correct

Quizzes are not a test of you. They show which ideas need another look, and explain every answer.

Close the loop, open the next

Finishing a lesson should make the next one feel natural, so progress carries you forward.

Why choose LearnLoop

  • Free to explore. Create an account to enroll and track progress.
  • Your progress is saved privately on your own device.
  • Short lessons with plain, friendly explanations.
  • Quizzes that explain why each answer is right or wrong.
  • Works on phones, tablets and computers.
